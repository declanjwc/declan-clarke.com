Rocket Airframe & Test Stand Structures

  • Structures team
  • Aug 2023 – now
  • Siemens NX, Ansys Fluent, Jira

Context

NC State's student liquid rocket program. I've worked on its structures team for three years, from model rockets to the engine test stand to the vehicle airframe.

What I did

Dynamic Model Engineer (2023–24), Test Stand Engineer & Funding Outreach (2024–25), and Airframe Engineer (2025–now): engine mount design support, funding outreach, and airframe material and sizing trade studies in NX.

Outcome

A test-stand engine mount designed to measure axial thrust, radial thrust, and engine weight from a single static fire; first place in the structures team's first-year challenge; and mentoring a first-year member through airframe analysis.

Airframe material and sizing trade studies · 2025 – now

NX modeling plus material and sizing trade studies for the liquid rocket airframe, including a carbon-composite trade study. I also mentored a first-year member through the analysis process.

One engine mount, three measurements · 2024 – 2025

Supported design of the engine mount for the lab's test stand so a single static fire produces three measurements:

  • Axial thrust, the main performance number
  • Radial thrust, the side loads from misalignment or off-axis thrust
  • Engine weight

Alongside the engineering, I worked on funding outreach for the team.

First year: dynamic model and a challenge win · 2023 – 2024

My first year on the team, and my introduction to Jira and Confluence. I won the structures team's first-year challenge with a model rocket.
