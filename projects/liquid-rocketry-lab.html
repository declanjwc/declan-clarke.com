The Liquid Rocketry Lab is NC State's student team designing, building, and testing liquid-propellant rocket engines and vehicles. I've been on the structures team since my first semester, in three roles.

Airframe Engineer · 2025 – now

NX modeling plus material and sizing trade studies for the liquid rocket airframe, including a carbon-composite trade study. I also mentored a first-year member through the analysis process.

Test Stand Engineer & Funding Outreach · 2024 – 2025

Supported design of the engine mount for the lab's engine test stand so it can measure:

  • Axial thrust, the primary performance number
  • Radial thrust, the side loads from misalignment or off-axis thrust
  • Engine weight

I also worked on funding outreach for the team.

Dynamic Model Engineer · 2023 – 2024

My first year on the team, and my introduction to Jira and Confluence. I won the structures team's first-year challenge with a passively steered model rocket.
