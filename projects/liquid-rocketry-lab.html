Test stand engine mount · 2024 – 2025

Supported design of the engine mount for the lab's test stand so a single static fire produces three measurements:

  • Axial thrust, the main performance number
  • Radial thrust, the side loads from misalignment or off-axis thrust
  • Engine weight

Alongside the engineering, I worked on funding outreach for the team.
